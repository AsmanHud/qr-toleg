**App name**

QR Töleg

**Short description**

Create and scan QR codes to make TMcell balance transfers easier.

**Full description**

QR Töleg makes TMcell balance transfers simpler by replacing manual phone-number entry with QR codes.

Create your personal QR code and show it to someone who wants to send you balance. To send balance, scan another QR Töleg code, enter the amount and review the transfer details.

QR Töleg prepares the transfer SMS for you. Before anything is sent, you can review the recipient, transfer amount, carrier fee, required balance and SMS message. The final SMS is opened in your messaging app, where you choose whether to send it.

QR Töleg works without an account, backend or cloud service. Your saved phone number stays on your device.

Features:
• Create a personal QR code for your TMcell number
• Scan QR Töleg codes with your camera
• Validate recipient numbers automatically
• Review the transfer amount and carrier fee
• Prepare the required TMcell transfer SMS
• Turkmen and English interface
• No account or cloud storage

QR Töleg does not send transfers automatically, check your account balance or verify that a transfer succeeds.

QR Töleg is an independent application. It is not an official TMcell application and is not affiliated with TMcell. Transfer availability, fees, limits and processing are controlled by TMcell.
