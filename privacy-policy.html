QR Töleg Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 16, 2026

QR Töleg is designed to work without an account, backend, analytics, ads, or cloud storage. This policy explains what information the app uses and how it stays under your control.

Information you provide

The app asks for your phone number to create your personal QR code. Your phone number is stored only on your device. QR Töleg does not send it to us or store it on our servers. You can remove it from the app at any time by resetting your phone number in Settings, or by uninstalling the app.

Camera access

QR Töleg requests camera access only so you can scan QR Töleg codes. The camera image is processed on your device. The app does not record, retain, or upload camera images or video.

QR codes and transfers

A QR Töleg code contains a phone number. When you scan a valid code, the app uses that number to prepare a balance-transfer SMS. QR Töleg opens your device's messaging app with the recipient number, transfer amount, and SMS destination filled in. You review the message and choose whether to send it. Your messaging app and mobile carrier handle the SMS under their own privacy practices.

Data collection and sharing

We do not collect, transmit, sell, or share personal information through QR Töleg. The app does not use analytics, advertising, tracking, or third-party data-collection services.

Children's privacy

QR Töleg does not knowingly collect personal information from children or from any other users.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy if the app's data practices change. The revised policy will be posted on this page with a new effective date.

Contact

For privacy questions, open an issue in the QR Töleg GitHub repository.

QR Töleg is an independent app and is not affiliated with or endorsed by TMcell.
